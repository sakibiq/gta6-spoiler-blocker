# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-18)

First release.

- Blurs (with a **Show** button) or hides GTA 6 videos, Shorts, posts, playlists and the GTA VI game card on
  home, search, sidebar, channel and notification lists.
- Blurs comments that mention GTA 6.
- Pauses and hides autoplay/hover previews that play over a blocked video.
- Holds a GTA 6 video at 0:00 and covers the page, with **Go back** and **Watch anyway**.
- Editable spoiler words, with spacing and spelling variants matched automatically (`GTA6`, `gta-6`, `#GTA6`).
- Settings popup with a live count of blocked items on the current page; settings sync across your Chrome profile.
