# Privacy

GTA 6 Spoiler Blocker does not collect, send or sell any data.

- It runs only on `youtube.com` and `m.youtube.com`, and only reads text that is already on the page
  (video titles, channel names, comments) to decide what to blur.
- Your settings (on/off, blur or hide, your spoiler words) are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`, so Chrome
  can sync them between your own signed-in browsers. Nothing else is stored.
- It makes no network requests, has no analytics and no remote code.
- The only permission it asks for is `storage`.
